# DHH FM product notes

## Voice

Playful, compact, and factual. The joke is that the station is always on—not
that its subject deserves harassment. Never infer private analytics or label
public engagement as reach.

## Panel

- Animated station ident and ON AIR state.
- Rolling 24-hour totals for posts, replies, and quotes/reposts.
- Feed filters: all, posts, replies.
- Public engagement totals when supplied by the configured source.
- Open, reply, and copy-link actions.
- Honest stale/offline and partial-history states.

## Data principles

- Prefer the official X API.
- Permit a user-configured RSS/Nitter-compatible endpoint as a limited fallback.
- Never collect browser cookies or X passwords.
- Cache normalized public posts locally.
- Never manufacture reach estimates.

## Future ideas

- Signals-per-hour sparkline.
- Busiest broadcast hour.
- Quiet-period and activity-streak copy.
- Media thumbnails.
- Optional desktop notification when the station returns after extended silence.
